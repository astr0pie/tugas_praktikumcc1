Who is Jinx?

Jinx is a character from the animated series Arcane, set in the world of League of Legends. She was once named Powder, a little girl who grew up in Zaun alongside her older sister, Vi.

After everything that happened to her in the undercity, Powder became Jinx: long blue braids, endless explosions, and walls covered in her scribbles.

The story revolves around two twin cities, the glittering Piltover and the gritty Zaun, and two sisters torn apart by them.
